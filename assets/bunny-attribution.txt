Woven-bunny entrance experiment

Reference: the user's calendar photo of a colorful woven Stanford bunny.
Paper: Weaving Geodesic Foliations, Josh Vekhter, Jiacheng Zhuo,
Luisa F. Gil Fandino, Qixing Huang, Etienne Vouga (2019).
https://doi.org/10.1145/3306346.3323043

Base mesh: meshes/bunny_coarser.obj from the paper's research-code repository:
https://github.com/the13fools/weaving-geodesic-foliations/blob/main/meshes/bunny_coarser.obj
Retrieved for the Stanford bunny silhouette. Vertex positions normalized and
normals computed for browser rendering.

The colorful strips in this entrance are a new procedural approximation inspired
by the calendar reference. They are not the paper's computed geodesic foliation,
original woven mesh, or a reproduction of its algorithms.

Implementation uses native WebGL with locally hosted assets. No network calls,
tracking, audio, or external runtime libraries. The original page remains usable
if JavaScript, WebGL, or the mesh is unavailable.
